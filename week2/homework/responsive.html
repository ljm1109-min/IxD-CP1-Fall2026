Stray Kids Members Profile

Stray Kids (스트레이 키즈) is a 8-member South Korean boy group under JYP Entertainment, consisting of Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, Han, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N. They debuted as a 9-member group on March 25, 2018, with their first mini album, I am NOT. The group was created through the survival program with the same name, Stray Kids. Woojin left the group on October 27, 2019. As of February 10, 2022, they are also signed under Republic Records.
